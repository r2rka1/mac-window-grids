Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Window Grids does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It makes no network requests, has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Accessibility: used only to read and change the position and size of windows, and to bring apps to the front, when you drag a window into a cell or switch layouts. The app does not read window contents or what you type.
  • Running apps: their names, identifiers and icons, to list them in the editor.

What the app stores

Your layouts (grid shapes and the identifiers of the apps you placed in them) and settings, in a file in the app's own Application Support folder on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party code or services.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/r2rka1/mac-window-grids.
